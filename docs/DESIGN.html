Do's and don'ts

✓ Do

  • Use a palette colour only to say where in the shop something is.
  • Get hierarchy from width and size; widen and set caps for signage.
  • Set numbers in data with tabular figures, aligned in a column.
  • Keep one primary button per screen, labelled with what happens.
  • Let the list run edge to edge on phones; keep rows at or under 44px.
  • Write plainly and actively, in sentence case.

✗ Don't

  • Don't build big-number statistic cards or a grid of identical cards.
  • Don't make fields pill-shaped, or give a field a button's silhouette.
  • Don't use green for "done" — green is produce. Completion is ink plus strikethrough.
  • Don't add blurred gradient blobs, glow, zero-padded numbers or shadow on every card.
  • Don't reach past weight 500 for body text, captions or inputs.
  • Don't animate anything but the check-off, and respect reduced motion.
